Careers and Job Opportunities

Change lives with people who will change yours

MaineHealth team members care for each other as much as they care for our communities. Every day, we help and support one another in our life-changing work.

And with a focus on continuing education and training, MaineHealth helps employees grow their careers to become the best version of themselves.

RNs, CNAs and MAs

MaineHealth is home to one of the most accomplished and talented clinical teams in the country. Are you a recently-graduated or experienced RN, CNA or MA? 

New to health care

MaineHealth welcomes job seekers of all experience levels. Learn about our training programs and rewarding health care career opportunities.

Explore all jobs

What makes MaineHealth such a great place to work? People like you. Our welcoming and respectful work culture honors people of all backgrounds and abilities.

Physician, APP and CRNA Careers

A MaineHealth career gives you the resources of Northern New England's largest integrated health system and the close personal connections of local community practice. We empower physicians and advanced practice professionals (APPs) to deliver compassionate, patient-centered care backed by clinical excellence, innovative research and academic collaboration.

Learn more about physician, APP and CRNA careers

The way life should be

A MaineHealth career comes with all the benefits of living in Northern New England. Supportive communities. Incredible natural beauty. Walkable cities with vibrant food, arts and music. And year-round access to unforgettable outdoor recreation.
