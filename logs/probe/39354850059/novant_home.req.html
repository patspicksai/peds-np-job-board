Enjoy the comprehensive benefits you deserve.

Taking care of people starts with our team members. That’s why Novant Health goes beyond offering all the benefits you'd expect from a large healthcare system, like health insurance and retirement. Our benefits are designed to take care of the whole you and keep you healthy — physically, emotionally, financially, and socially.

Expect competitive compensation.

Novant Health is committed to offering market-competitive rates, raises, and bonus opportunities. And, with signing bonuses and relocation assistance for some of our most in-demand positions, there has never been a better time to maximize your potential.

You choose what’s best for your family.

Pre-tax benefit selections include:
  • Health, dental, and vision insurance
  • Flexible spending accounts (FSA)
  • Life insurance
  • Supplemental life and disability insurance options
We also offer team members:
  • Episode of care services (bundled healthcare service package at a single, lower cost)
  • Specialty pharmacy services
  • Special pricing for Novant Health network providers and clinics

Grow your future.

With your 403(b) retirement account through Fidelity Investments, you have:
  • Pre-tax and after-tax contribution options
  • Dollar-for-dollar matching as soon as you start
  • Vesting in your matched contributions after 3 years of service (don’t have to be consecutive)

The time away from work you deserve

We understand that healthcare jobs can be demanding; that to deliver excellent care, you need to be able to rest, relax, care for your loved ones, and recover. That’s why we offer:

  • Flexible paid time off, starting at 16 hours per month with increases each year you work here
  • Paid parental, caregiver, and military leave
  • Physician paid leave

Find balance. Get rewarded.

We support wellness for the whole you — in every area of your life. Our total well-being program is designed to enhance your emotional wellness, human connections, financial resources, and physical health through tools and resources like:

  • Lifestyle programs (nutrition and fitness)
  • Incentive programs, such as for attending your annual physical
  • Mental health resources
  • Employee assistance program (EAP)
  • Employee fitness centers

Empowering you to thrive

Novant Health supports you in your pursuit to advance your career and achieve your personal goals.

  • Tuition reimbursement
  • Physician practice partnerships
  • Volunteer opportunities and matching
  • Discounts and perks

Military friendly

Novant Health champions the hiring of military service members and their spouses for our available healthcare jobs. Supporting veterans is not just a priority, but a mission that we carry out with our team members and our patients every day. Military service members provide innovative leadership and professional expertise as we work together to create a healthier future for all. Our core values — compassion, personal excellence, courage, and teamwork — are values veterans embody through their service.

Novant Health proudly offers military leave to support our team members who make the personal and professional sacrifice to serve in the U.S. Armed Forces.
