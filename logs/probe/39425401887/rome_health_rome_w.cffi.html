  • 10.16.26

    Rome Health offers walk-in screening mammograms at Turning Stone

    As the world celebrates Breast Cancer Awareness Month, Rome Health is making routine breast cancer screening more convenient with walk-in screening mammograms at Rome Health Convenient Care at Turning Stone beginning Oct. 19. Eligible adults 40 and older can walk in for a screening mammogram on Mondays and Tuesdays from 8 a.m. to 3:30 p.m. No appointment is needed.

  • 09.25.26

    Free Hernia Screening October 14

    Board-certified surgeon Pete Del Pino, MD, will offer free hernia screenings from 5 to 7 p.m. Wednesday, October 14, at Oneida Surgical Group, 357 Genesee St., Suite 3, Oneida.  Appointments are limited and may be scheduled by calling 315-338-7209. During the free screening, Dr. Del Pino will evaluate symptoms, answer questions and determine whether additional testing or treatment is recommended.

  • 08.04.26

    Rome Health Recognized Among Nation's Hospitals with Zero MRSA Infection Rate

    Rome Health has been recognized as one of just 312 hospitals nationwide to achieve a methicillin-resistant Staphylococcus aureus (MRSA) bloodstream infection rate of zero, according to the latest Healthcare-Associated Infections Hospital dataset released by the Centers for Medicare & Medicaid Services (CMS).
