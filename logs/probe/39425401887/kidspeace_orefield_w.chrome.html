Gio’s Story

For more than a decade, KidsPeace has provided a place where kids with autism are welcomed and cared for during their summer break from school.  The program, called Sarah’s Smile, has proved to be very popular with families and youth – and it may not have any bigger fan than a young man named Gio.

The Story of Oasis

It began as a quick way to reduce acute stress among KidsPeace associates in their daily work – but Oasis Services has expanded into a sophisticated offering of tools and techniques that address the effects of not just acute stress, but chronic stress as well.

Piper’s Story

Meet Piper – a teenager whose love for life and self-expression has given the therapeutic experts in KidsPeace’s Intensive Behavioral Health Services a new perspective on what it means to be “helping kids live their best lives.”
