Thank you for your interest in Meritus Health

Meritus Health is proud to be the largest health system in Western Maryland, providing a wide range of inpatient and outpatient medical services to our community. We prioritize our team members, considering them the heart of our organization. If you are seeking an inclusive workplace, collaborative teams and an environment that promotes goal attainment and professional growth, we invite you to join us. Our daily work makes a difference in the lives of our patients and our community.

At Meritus, we believe in a collaborative and caring work environment. Interactions are an opportunity to learn, listen and to be there for one another. Therefore, we provide warm welcomes, hospitality-driven closures, and are always Happy to Help.

Frequently Asked Questions

How can I check the status of my application?

Applicants are notified directly by email once a decision has been made. To ensure fairness and efficiency, our Team Member Services team is unable to provide application status updates by phone or email.

I haven’t received an update yet. Does that mean I am no longer being considered?

Not necessarily. Some positions require more time to review applications. Please continue to monitor your email (including your spam/junk folder), as all communication about your application will be sent there.

Who can I contact if I have other questions?

For questions not related to application status, you may email us at careers@meritushealth.com. Our TMS team will respond as appropriate.

Where can I find more information about open positions?

You can view and apply for all current openings on our Careers Page.
