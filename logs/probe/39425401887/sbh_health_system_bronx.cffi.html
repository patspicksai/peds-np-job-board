We’re seeking health care leaders

Join our team and be part of the life changing care at SBH

SBH makes a difference in the lives of everyone in the Bronx. We are the safety net of care for the Bronx community. Everyone, from doctors to administration to hospital staff, makes a difference here. The impact they make on people’s lives is one of the many reason why most employees stay at SBH once they’ve come to SBH. You can make a difference too.

A hospital system that reflects  the community it serves

The Bronx is a diverse community. And our staff reflects the unique make up of our community. All races, genders, religions, and cultures are respected and valued here. SBH has a deep understanding of the various needs and challenges that our community has. And our staff is able to serve those needs through our rich diversity of people we employ
