Prenatal Tour
calendar_today
October 28, 2026
schedule
12:00 - 2:00pm
location_on

Mission Hospital

During this hospital tour, expectant parents will become familiar with the spaces and routines they'll encounter during their birth experience. The tour begins in the Labor & Delivery unit, where your instructor will walk you through what to expect when you arrive. You'll see a labor and delivery room, learn about available comfort measures and equipment, and have the chance to ask questions.

Next, you'll visit the postpartum unit to get a sense of where you'll recover and bond with your baby after birth. We'll review newborn care procedures, rooming-in practices, and what to bring for both you and your baby. The tour also covers practical details—parking, after-hours entry and where to check in-so you can feel confident and prepared when the time comes.

This session is designed to help reduce anxiety, build familiarity with your care environment, and empower you to make informed decisions for a positive birth experience.

Please register separately for each guest attending.
