Jobs at MWPH

At Mt. Washington Pediatric Hospital, what sets us apart is our mission-driven culture, strong interdisciplinary collaboration, and the opportunity to build meaningful, long-term relationships with children and their families. Our team members are supported as they work to make a real difference in an environment where their expertise is respected, their voices are valued, and their professional growth is supported. We also believe in supporting our employees both professionally and personally. Our total rewards package goes beyond competitive base pay and includes a comprehensive range of benefits such as robust health coverage, retirement contributions, generous paid time off, tuition assistance, and ongoing opportunities for learning and career development.

When you join MWPH, you’re not just starting a job—you’re becoming part of a community dedicated to improving the lives of children and families.

Message From Our President

Mt. Washington Pediatric Hospital's mission of caring for children with limited resources and many needs is truly inspiring.
