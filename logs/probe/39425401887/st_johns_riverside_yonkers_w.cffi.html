Welcome To St. John’s Riverside Hospital

St. John’s Riverside Hospital is a comprehensive network of health care services for the Yonkers and Rivertown communities of Hasting-on-Hudson, Ardsley, Dobbs Ferry and Irvington. St. John’s Riverside Hospital’s goal is to inspire the community to take positive steps toward better health. Elevating overall wellness while introducing new and simple ways to keep your health in check, drives us. Leading expert medical and nursing staff will challenge you to incorporate new habits and remedy health issues with the latest treatments and most advanced technology.

Inspiring the best health keeps our Community Strong.

Specialties

As a community hospital, it is critical to focus on the health and well-being of its community members. You do not have to travel to NYC for the finest medical care, as generations have trusted their care to us as a leading medical facility right in the heart of the community. We have designed specialist teams to answer your health needs, and many of these professionals come from the same community we serve. These collaborative teams are built on solid training from top US and world-renowned schools and hospitals and have vast experience in many progressive areas, such as harnessing new robotic approaches.

Programs

Understanding the needs of the community has driven us to develop holistic, comprehensive wellness programs focused on meeting specific wellness gaps. These programs help the community solve medical needs, on the job, transitioning from hospital to home or rehab, nutritionally, and with the safe disposal of medical waste.
