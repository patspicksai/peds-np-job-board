Explore Penn Highlands Career Opportunities

Penn Highlands Healthcare provides access to the region's best hospitals, with each facility being the largest employer in its hometown. Clinical and non-clinical positions all play a role, and every member of our team touches the lives of our patients. Our positions offer work-life balance with time to enjoy the charming aspects of the area that are often overlooked - easy commutes, friendly neighbors, and plenty of space to explore.

For the fifth consecutive year, Penn Highlands Healthcare received recognition as one of Pennsylvania Business Central's Top 100 Organizations.
