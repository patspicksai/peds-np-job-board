Personalized care for all patients in Dulles, Virginia

Our hospital, conveniently located on Route 50 in Loudoun County provides emergency, medical, mental health, surgical and women's health services. As a vital member of the HCA Healthcare System, we share the strength of more than 160 hospitals including our sister hospital down the street, Reston Hospital Center, and across the United States and England.
About Our Hospital

Why people choose us

Our hospital is committed to offering advanced healthcare services with a compassionate, patient-focused approach in the Dulles community. The hospital has garnered recognition for high quality patient and surgical care and consistently earns high marks in patient experience.

StonesSprings Noon Express Birthing Suites Tour
calendar_today
October 27, 2026
schedule
12:00 - 1:00pm
location_on

StoneSprings Hospital Center

Having a new baby is exciting!! We want you to feel as comfortable as possible when it's time to deliver your baby. Join us as we offer you a IN Person tour of our Labor and Delivery Unit featuring our spacious birthing suites and our Mother-Baby suites. In addition to viewing the Birthing Suites, we will have a Q&A session hosted by one of our valued team members to answer your specific questions in regards to our facility and your upcoming experience with us! We ask that you view the video tour URL's, that will be sent with your invitation, prior to the In Person tour and the Q&A session to better guide our conversation.

The Q&A session may have up to 12 couples participating at once this allows for a group exchange of information. Come see the beautiful unit you will welcome your new little family member into the world!

Labor of Love Express - Childbirth Education Class
calendar_today
October 27, 2026
schedule
6:00 - 9:00pm
location_on

StoneSprings Hospital Center - Medical Office Building

Do you know what to expect when you go into labor? Grab your support person and join me for our IN Person Childbirth Education class taught by our SSHC Certified Childbirth Educator/Nurse.

Topics covered include what to expect from our L&D unit at StoneSprings Hospital, signs of true vs. false labor, stages of labor, options for pain relief, relaxation techniques, delivery options, interventions and so much more!

This class focuses heavily on a vaginal delivery, but briefly touches on an unplanned cesarean section. If you are planning on a scheduled cesarean section, please let me know and I will provide you with all the information you need ahead of time, and a video tour of our Operating Rooms at SSHC.
