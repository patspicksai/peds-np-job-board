Awards and Recognitions

WATERBURY HOSPITAL EARNS NATIONAL DISTINCTIONS FOR TREATMENT OF DIABETES, HEART ARRYTHMIA AND PNEUMONIA

Waterbury Hospital has been named a High Performing Hospital by U.S. News & World Report for the quality of care it provides to patients with Diabetes, Heart Arrhythmia, and Pneumonia, according to the recently released 2025–26 Best Hospitals rankings.

UCONN HEALTH COMMUNITY NETWORK MEDICAL GROUP PRIMARY AND PEDIATRIC LOCATIONS ACHIEVE PATIENT-CENTERED MEDICAL HOME RECOGNITION

Six UConn Health Network Medical Group primary care locations in Middlebury, Naugatuck, Woodbury, and Watertown have achieved Patient-Centered Medical Home Recognition by the National Committee for Quality Assurance.

 

UCONN HEALTH WATERBURY HOSPTIAL HONORS AREA FIRST RESPONDERS

UConn Health Waterbury Hospital honored several first responders and EMS agencies during its annual EMS Awards Banquet, recognizing outstanding EMTs, paramedics, dispatchers, educators, EMS leaders, hospital staff and emergency service organizations for their dedication and service to the community. Honored teams included agencies that responded to a major school bus accident in Southbury in February, teams that responded to a gas leak in Naugatuck in November 2025, and Naugatuck responders who saved the life of a man suffering a heart attack.
