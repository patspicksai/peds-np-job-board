Princeton Health

Princeton Health is dedicated to providing exceptional, comprehensive care across Central New Jersey, from primary and specialty services to advanced treatments and wellness programs. As part of Penn Medicine, we offer leading-edge medical expertise with a personalized touch to meet the diverse needs of our community.

About us

Princeton Health opened in 1919 in a small farmhouse on Witherspoon Street. Since then, we have grown to become Penn Medicine Princeton Health. Backed by Penn Medicine expertise, we are a comprehensive health care provider, offering advanced community care while educating future physicians.

Our locations

Princeton Medical Center

Princeton Medical Center has been a leading teaching hospital for more than 50 years. We combine the advanced care and state-of-the-art technology of an academic medical center with a personalized, community-centered approach.

Princeton House Behavioral Health

Princeton House has been a regional leader in behavioral health care for more than 50 years. We provide 24/7 inpatient treatment and multiple outpatient programs.

Outpatient health care in central New Jersey

Our conveniently located outpatient centers offer a range of services, from primary and specialty care to behavioral health, diagnostic procedures, lab tests, and more.

Caring for our community

Princeton Health is committed to enhancing community health and well-being through education, screenings, and support tailored to all ages and backgrounds. Through collaborations with local organizations, we empower residents to take charge of their health needs.

Princeton Medical Center Foundation

Charitable support has been the bedrock of Princeton Health since Princeton philanthropist Moses Taylor Pyne donated the modest farmhouse where Princeton Hospital opened. Today, generous giving remains the most important source of funding for clinical equipment and new facilities.

How to donate

The Princeton Medical Center Foundation was established in 1978 to financially support and promote the mission of Princeton Health. In recent years, donations have enabled us to completely renovate the Maternal-Child Health Program unit, expand the emergency department, invest in new mammography machines, establish the cardiac catheterization laboratory, and more.

Transforming Episode Accountability Model (TEAM)

Princeton Health is participating in a Centers for Medicare & Medicaid Services mandatory program called the “Transforming Episode Accountability Model”. A CMS model is a trial program that tests how health care can be improved for people with Medicare and Medicaid.
