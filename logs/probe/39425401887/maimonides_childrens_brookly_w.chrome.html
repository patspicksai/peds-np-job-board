My Bklyn. My Care.

Brooklyn's destination for outstanding healthcare

For over 112 years, we’ve been providing world-class care for a borough that’s unlike anywhere else in the world. If you’re from Brooklyn, chances are you know someone who we’ve helped. Our doctors, nurses, and staff are your neighbors. And with a brand new ER in Bay Ridge and ratings that rank us No. 1 in surgical care in all of New York, we’re not going anywhere.

Brooklyn's largest standalone children's ED now open

This new iteration of the Children’s Emergency Department offers an expanded space, with 32 treatment areas, separate ambulance and walk-in entrances, and advanced on-site imaging, including X-ray, ultrasound, and CT scanning for rapid diagnosis of emergency conditions. We designed it to be child-friendly, with a warm, calming environment that meets both the medical and emotional needs of young patients and their families.

Maimonides Doctors Clinton Hill Pavilion brings award-winning healthcare to central Brooklyn

Located at the corner of Vanderbilt and Atlantic Avenues, Maimonides Doctors Clinton Hill Pavilion consolidates three practices—Maimonides Doctors Sunrise Medical Group, Maimonides Obstetrics and Gynecology Women’s Health, and Maimonides Multispecialty Care—into one modern facility designed for convenience, comfort, and coordinated healthcare.

Expanding services to the Midwood Community

Maimonides Midwood Community Hospital offers innovative services including:

  • Advanced cardiac care, with
    state-of-the-art catheterization lab, PCI, and electrophysiology for screening and diagnosis of heart conditions
  • Interventional radiology, which allows doctors to use MRI imaging technology to guide minimally invasive surgeries
