Northern Light Mercy Hospital

Northern Light Mercy Hospital provides nationally recognized orthopedic and surgical care in Portland, Maine, along with comprehensive healthcare for the Greater Portland community. Our orthopedic services include hip and knee replacement, shoulder surgery, sports medicine procedures, spine surgery, and advanced minimally invasive surgery. Mercy is certified by The Joint Commission for hip, knee, and spine surgery. We also provide cardiovascular care, emergency care, women’s health, maternity care, cancer care, general surgery, primary care, imaging, and a wide range of specialty services.

Surgical Services

Northern Light Mercy Hospital is nationally recognized for excellence in surgical outcomes, safety, and patient experience, and we are known throughout Maine for the personalized and compassionate care we provide.

Mercy’s surgical practices offer a wide range of services, including spine surgery, orthopedics, breast care, women's health, general surgery, and ear, nose, and throat care. When you become our patient, your care team will support and guide you throughout the process – from consultation to surgery, successful discharge, and home care. We invite you to view a patient experience video to learn more about a typical surgical patient’s journey at Mercy Hospital.

Partnering in the care of our community

Mercy Hospital partners with community physicians and practices that perform surgical procedures in our facilities, including those that provide the following services:

Orthopedic Surgery

Mercy Hospital is accredited by The Joint Commission and has been certified for hip, knee, and spine surgery since 2012 for high-quality care. Services: Sports medicine, arthroscopic procedures, joint replacements, fracture care, shoulder surgery, hand and wrist surgery, elbow surgery, hip and knee replacement, rotator cuff repair, labral tear repair, meniscal debridement and repair, ligament and tendon repair, total and partial knee replacement, total shoulder replacement and reverse shoulder replacement approaches, and femoroacetabular impingement treatment.
