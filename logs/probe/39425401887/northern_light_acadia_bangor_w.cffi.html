How to apply for a position with Acadia Hospital

To apply for a job with Northern Light Acadia Hospital visit the Northern Light Health Careers Page and follow these steps:

 

If you already know the position number:

  1. Click the ‘hamburger’ menu in the upper left corner of the Careers portal.
  2. Click “Job Search”
  3. Enter the job number in the field for Job ID
  4. Hit Enter or click "Search"

If you do not have a position number and would like to search for all available positions at Acadia Hospital:
  1. Click the ‘hamburger’ menu in the upper left corner of the Careers portal.
  2. Click “Job Search”
  3. Enter Acadia Hospital in the field for Company
  4. Hit Enter or click "Search"
In both instances you may also search by keyword.

You will need to register and create a profile to apply for a position at Northern Light Health.
