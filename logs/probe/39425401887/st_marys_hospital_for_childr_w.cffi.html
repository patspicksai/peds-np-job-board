Where big hearts help little patients

St. Mary’s Healthcare System for Children is committed to improving the health and quality of life for children, young adults, and families with medical complexity and special healthcare needs.
More About Our Mission
Featured Stories

Every Story Matters

Each little patient and young adult at St. Mary’s Healthcare System for Children has a unique story of courage, hope, and perseverance.

View All Stories

Adalynn

Adalynn continues to make tremendous strides each day and is currently walking and utilizing language stimulation cause-and-effect toys to help her communicate more.
Read Adalynn's Story

Matthew

Matthew was diagnosed with Sly Syndrome in 2002 and was transferred to St. Mary’s Hospital for Children soon after being diagnosed, so he could receive intensive care and monitoring he needed day-to-day that he was unable to receive at home.
Read Matthew's Story

Nate

Nathaniel (Nate) arrived at St. Mary’s on a cold New York day in February 2023, after his mother made the difficult decision to transition him from home to the hospital.
Read Nate's Story

Elias

After extraordinary measures and being placed on life support, Lukas spent three months in the NICU before being transferred to St. Mary’s Hospital for Children, receiving a wide range of critical services
Read Elias's Story

Adiv

Adiv is a 10-year-old boy with dysautonomia, a medical condition that prevents his autonomic nervous system from working properly.
Read Adiv's Story
