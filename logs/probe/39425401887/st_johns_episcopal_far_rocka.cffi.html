It’s a New Day in the Rockaways!

Episcopal Health Services Inc. (EHS) is growing, and with this new growth, new opportunities are waiting
for you. We are an expansive healthcare delivery system with a vision for the future, our communities
and the patients we serve! EHS is dedicated to providing quality patient and family-centered care, and
health equity to all. It’s an exciting time to be a part of our dynamic team.
EHS is committed to Health Equity/Social Impact and professional and personal growth.

The journey begins with you, be a part of the nurturing of others!

Thank you for your interest in joining our team, we look forward to hearing from you soon!
