In just over 50 years, UConn Health has evolved to encompass more research endeavors, to provide more ways to access our superior care, and to innovate both practical medicine and our methods of educating the practitioners of tomorrow.

UConn Health and UConn Athletics Announce Expansion to Longtime Partnership

Beginning with the 2026-27 season, the UConn Health logo will appear on the uniforms of UConn Husky Men’s and Women’s Basketball for home and away games in the preseason and regular season, reflecting the continued growth of a relationship rooted in a shared pursuit of excellence – excellence in healthcare and excellence on the court.
