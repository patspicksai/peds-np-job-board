Careers

Thank you for visiting Nathan Littauer’s Careers page! We strive to hire individuals who live up to the mission and ICARE Standards of our organization. Use the job search tool below to learn what exciting career opportunities exist at Nathan Littauer!

At Nathan Littauer, the difference is YOU.

We are a small rural hospital located in the scenic foothills of the Adirondacks within a 45-minute commute from the Saratoga Region and a 60-minute commute of the Albany Region. We are a family-oriented community with excellent schools and year-round recreation. So, if you are looking to make a big impact in a small community, then Littauer could be your professional home.

Nathan Littauer employees understand that our success is predicated on each and every patient interaction. We all share in this crucial responsibility. Everything we do is to ensure our patients have the best possible outcome at Nathan Littauer. When you join the Littauer Team you are committing to provide compassionate, quality health care while remaining true to our ICARE Standards.

To learn more about the Qualities of a Littauer Employee and the Benefits of working at Littauer click on the links. To learn how some of our employees are making a difference, check out what they had to say on YouTube.

Applying to Littauer

If you prefer to fill out a paper application, please stop by Human Resources Monday – Friday between the hours of 8:00am and 4:30pm.  We are located at 99 E. State Street Gloversville NY.
Please note: All applications remain on file for one (1) year. It is not necessary to submit multiple applications during that year. Should you find another position that is of interest to you after you submit an application, please call (518) 773-5404.
