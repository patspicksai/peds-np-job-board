Start Your Purpose-Driven Career

Build your career at Temple Health, one of Greater Philadelphia’s leading academic health systems. Teams across clinical, research, operational and support roles work together to deliver exceptional patient care, advance groundbreaking research and bring innovative treatments to patients across research-focused and community-based settings.

Current Employee Career Opportunities

Temple Health employees have access to internal opportunities across clinical, research, operational and support roles. Current team members can search open positions and apply directly.

Internal Employee Job Search

Growing Your Career

“Being an academic medical center, Temple has a little bit of everything. Every day, you have the opportunity to learn new things – and there are tons of options for people at all stages of their career.” – Stacy Bouikidis, MSN, CRNA | TUH – Main Campus

Learn More About What Sets Temple Health Apart

Nursing Careers at Temple Health

Temple Health’s Magnet-designated nurses are empowered to lead, innovate and deliver compassionate care. Supported by mentorship, continued education and research opportunities, they make a meaningful impact on patients and the future of nursing.

Learn More About Nursing at Temple Health

A Leader in Cancer Care and Research

As part of Temple Health, Fox Chase Cancer Center offers employees the opportunity to work alongside nationally recognized experts in cancer treatment, research, and innovation. From groundbreaking clinical trials and laboratory discoveries to compassionate patient care, Fox Chase is helping shape the future of cancer medicine while creating meaningful career opportunities across clinical, research, and support roles.

Making an Impact Across the Community

At Temple Health, you can be part of something bigger. With dedicated teams across academic hospitals, Temple satellite sites, community practices, and more, your work makes a real difference strengthening communities and support a healthier future across Greater Philadelphia.

Faculty Physician Careers at the Lewis Katz School of Medicine at Temple University

Temple Health is an educational partner with the Lewis Katz School of Medicine at Temple University. Physicians applying for work within Temple University's Health System must apply through the medical school.
