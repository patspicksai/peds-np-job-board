About Us

Commonwealth Health is one of the state’s largest healthcare networks. While we provide care at facilities all over the Northeastern Pennsylvania map, our focus remains on patients.

What should you expect from this network?

  • Certified Stroke Centers
  • Certified Chest Pain Centers
  • Make a doctor's appointment online, anytime
  • Improved access to specialty care locally
  • Most major insurances are accepted, including Geisinger Health Plan
