Why Oswego Health?

The quality of care defines Oswego Health we provide. The mission and commitment championed by all our skilled caregivers throughout our health system have ensured the well-being of this community since our doors opened as a small hospital in 1881.

We Care is our pledge. It’s a mantra that every one of us carries as a teammate of this healthcare system. Simply said, it’s what we do, and defines who we are. We care about the little things. We care about making connections. We care about collaboration. We care about what matters most – YOU.

As we build upon our 140+year tradition as an independent healthcare system and continue to lead the transformation of local care, we are looking for champions to join our team. Those that are driven, motivated and passionate and willing to accept our pledge to care for the community!
