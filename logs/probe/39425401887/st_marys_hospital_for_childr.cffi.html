Join Our Team

For over 150 years, our holistic approach has helped our patients beyond the beds and into the community. We are committed to improving the health and quality of life for children, young adults and families with medical complexities and special healthcare needs. 

Join our dedicated team and make a lasting impact on the lives of children and their families. Explore rewarding career opportunities and be part of our mission to build brighter futures every day!

You will receive specialized training, competitive pay, and benefits and join a 1,000-strong community of people who share your values and generous spirit. Here, your big heart will touch the lives of our little patients—and the families who love them—in ways that will be more rewarding than you ever thought possible.

Independent Home Care Contractor Opportunities

Join the St. Mary’s Healthcare System for Children home rehabilitation community in an independent contractor capacity with St. Mary’s Home Care, a special needs certified home health agency (CHHA). Providing care for patients from newborn to 44 years of age, St. Mary’s home care focuses on improving their quality of life from the comfort of home.

Inclusion & Diversity

What Makes Us Different Makes Us Stronger. Our commitment to fostering a compassionate, inclusive environment welcomes children, families, staff, and all individuals, building unity within our community.

We value a workplace where everyone is treated with respect and dignity, and where contributions are enhanced by diversity. Our commitment to inclusion is reflected in our support for employees of all ages, and we are proud to be part of the AARP Employee Pledge Program, a nationwide initiative promoting diverse, high-performing organizations.

Nursing Opportunities

At St. Mary’s, our nurses empower patients and families through compassionate, team-based care. Working alongside physicians, therapists, and social workers, they provide education, support, and hands-on training to help children gain confidence and independence. We offer flexible nursing opportunities across Inpatient Care, Home Care, Full-time, Part-time, and Per Diem roles. Ready to make an impact? Explore our opportunities below!

Search Nursing Opportunities

Clinical Opportunities

At St. Mary’s, our expert clinical staff—therapists, physicians, and medical professionals—play a vital role in patient recovery, addressing physical, cognitive, and developmental needs.

We offer Clinical opportunities in Physical Therapy, Speech Therapy, Occupational Therapy, Inpatient Services, and Home Care. Ready to make a difference? Explore our openings below!
