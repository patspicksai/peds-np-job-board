										<content:encoded><![CDATA[<p><strong>Position Summary</strong></p>
<p>The Nurse Practitioner (NP) assigned to the Women’s Health Center will provide comprehensive, patient-centered care to women receiving obstetrical and gynecologic services, with a primary focus on patients followed by Maternal-Fetal Medicine (MFM) and patients enrolled in our elavateHER programs, (ie. Diabetes Centering Program).</p>
<p>The NP will work collaboratively with Maternal-Fetal Medicine physicians, general OB/GYN physicians, certified nurse-midwives, nurses, diabetes educators, nutritionists, social workers, and other members of the multidisciplinary healthcare team. The NP will assist in the management, education, coordination, and follow-up of patients with high-risk pregnancies, particularly those affected by pregestational diabetes, gestational diabetes, and other maternal or fetal medical conditions.</p>
<p><strong>Core Responsibilities</strong></p>
<ul>
<li>Provide clinical care and follow-up for patients enrolled in the Maternal-Fetal Medicine service.</li>
<li>Obtain comprehensive histories and perform appropriate physical assessments.</li>
<li>Review laboratory results, medications, and other relevant clinical information.</li>
<li>Assist with the management and coordination of high-risk pregnancies under established protocols and in collaboration with MFM physicians.</li>
<li>Identify changes in a patient’s clinical condition and communicate concerns promptly to the appropriate physician.</li>
<li>Reinforce individualized plans of care developed by the MFM team.</li>
<li>Coordinate referrals, consultations, diagnostic testing, and follow-up appointments.</li>
<li>Provide patient education regarding high-risk pregnancy conditions, medications, testing, and recommended follow-up.</li>
<li>Facilitate communication between MFM, the Women’s Health Center, Labor and Delivery and other specialty services as appropriate.</li>
</ul>
<p>Diabetes Centering Program</p>
<ul>
<li>Serve as a key clinical provider for pregnant patients enrolled in the Diabetes Centering Program.</li>
<li>Participate in and help facilitate group prenatal care sessions for patients with gestational or pregestational diabetes.</li>
<li>Review blood glucose logs and continuous glucose monitoring information when applicable.</li>
<li>Provide education regarding glucose monitoring, medication adherence, nutrition, physical activity, and the impact of diabetes on pregnancy.</li>
<li>Collaborate with physicians, diabetes educators, registered dietitians, nursing staff, and other team members to support individualized diabetes management plans.</li>
<li>Reinforce signs and symptoms that require medical evaluation and ensure patients understaffed when and how to contact their healthcare team.</li>
<li>Monitor patient participation and assist with outreach to patients who miss appointments or require additional follow-up.</li>
<li>Promote patient engagement, self-management, and adherence to the recommended plan of care.</li>
<li>Assist in tracking clinical outcomes and program measures related to the Diabetes Centering Program.</li>
</ul>
<p><strong>Women’s Health Center Responsibilities</strong></p>
<ul>
<li>Provide prenatal and postpartum care within the NPs scope of practice and established departmental protocol.</li>
<li>Perform health assessments, order appropriate diagnostic studies, and review results.</li>
<li>Prescribe medications in accordance with state regulations, hospital policies, clinical privileges, and collaborative practice requirements.</li>
<li>Provide counseling and education related to pregnancy, postpartum care, contraception, preventive health, and chronic disease management.</li>
<li>Maintain accurate, timely, and complete documentation in the electronic medical record.</li>
<li>Participate in multidisciplinary case discussions, care coordination meetings, and quality improvement activities.</li>
<li>Support efforts to reduce barriers to care and promote continuity for high-risk patients.</li>
<li>Participate in development and implementation of clinical workflows, protocols, and patient education initiatives.</li>
<li>Ensure care is delivered in accordance with hospital policies, regulatory requirements, evidence-based standards, and departmental guidelines.</li>
</ul>
