Nurse Practitioner (APRN) or Physician Assistant (PA) UroGYN FT
Req Id:  64971

Full-Time | Bergen County, NJ | 10 Minutes from NYC

 

Englewood Health is seeking a highly skilled and motivated Advanced Practice Nurse (APN) or Physician Assistant (PA) to join our dynamic Department of Surgery. This advanced practice provider will work collaboratively with surgeons, residents, nursing staff, and multidisciplinary teams to deliver exceptional care to patients with complex conditions in our expanding UroGYN practice.

 

About the Opportunity

 

  • Our growing Urogynecology practice is dedicated to providing exceptional, patient-centered care for women experiencing pelvic floor disorders, urinary incontinence, pelvic organ prolapse, and related conditions. 
    The Advanced Practice Provider will play an integral role in patient evaluation, treatment planning, education, and ongoing management of urogynecologic conditions. The ideal candidate is passionate about women's health, possesses strong clinical skills, and thrives in a team-oriented practice setting.
  • GYN Onc/UroTeam: Three (3) Providers, One (1) Nurse Practitioner.
  • Schedule: Days, Monday - Friday
  • Comprehensive benefits package: including medical/vision/dental/prescription, FSA, retirement plan, life insurance, malpractice insurance, generous paid time off (PTO), tuition assistance, Employee Assistance Program (EAP), Fitness discount/Gym reimbursement program, and more…

 

What your day will look like

 

  • Conduct comprehensive patient assessments, histories, and physical examinations.
  • Diagnose and manage patients with urogynecologic and pelvic floor disorders.
  • Develop and implement treatment plans in collaboration with physicians.
  • Provide patient education regarding diagnoses, treatment options, procedures, and preventive care.
  • Perform office-based procedures as appropriate and within scope of practice.
  • Review diagnostic testing and communicate results to patients.
  • Document clinical encounters accurately and efficiently in the electronic medical record (EMR).
  • Coordinate care with referring physicians and multidisciplinary healthcare providers.
  • Participate in quality improvement initiatives and practice growth efforts.
  • Maintain compliance with all clinical, regulatory, and organizational standards.

 

Qualifications

 

All Candidates

  • 2-4 years of experience in Women's Health, Gynecology, Urology, Urogynecology, or surgical specialty preferred
  • DEA and NJ CDS registration
  • Current BLS and ACLS certification

 

Advanced Practice Nurse

  • Master’s or Doctorage degree in Nursing from an accredited program
  • National Board certificatioin is required
  • Current NJ Professional Nurse License
  • Current NJ Nurse Practitioner License

 

Physician Assistant

  • Master’s degree from an accredited Physician Assistant program
  • Current NJ Physician Assistant License
  • Current National Commission on Certification of Physician Assistants (NCCPA) Certification

 

Why Join Us

 

  • Top-ranked #2 Hospital in New Jersey
  • #1 in Bergen County for Quality, Safety & Patient Experience
  • Just 10 minutes from New York City
  • Access to world-class dining, culture, entertainment and recreation
