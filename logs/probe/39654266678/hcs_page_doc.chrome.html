** Exciting Employment Opportunity for Advanced Practice Providers! ** 

Join the dynamic team at CaroMont Medical Group, located just a short drive from the vibrant city of Charlotte, NC—one of the fastest-growing cities in the nation! We are seeking Board Certified Advanced Practice Providers (NP/PA) to work across our Primary Care Service Line which includes our Family Medicine and Urgent Care Clinics. 

** Position Highlights: ** 

  • Join a collaborative and supportive team environment with a strong commitment to quality care. 

  • Deliver full-spectrum care, including Pediatrics. 

  • Practice in modern, well-equipped facilities with access to CaroMont’s extensive resources and network. 

  • Crosstrain and provide coverage for CaroMont Urgent Care, training provided. 

  • Family Medicine & Urgent Care Shifts 

  • Cover one weekend shift per month in Urgent Care 

**Requirements** 

  • Master’s or Doctoral degree from accredited NP or PA program 

  • Board Certified FNP or PA-C 

  • Active NC License & DEA and Active SC License & DEA 

  • BLS/ACLS certification 

** Why CaroMont? ** 

CaroMont Health is an independent, not-for-profit healthcare system that prides itself on delivering exceptional care through a 476-bed hospital, brand-new satellite campus in Belmont, NC which opened its doors to the public in January 2025, extensive primary care, specialty physician network, and an accredited Cancer Center. We are deeply committed to enhancing the health and well-being of our community.  

** What We Offer: ** 

- Competitive Compensation and Benefits Package: Enjoy a comprehensive benefits package that includes a productivity bonus, CME, Paid Time Off, Generous Benefits, and Malpractice Coverage.   

- Supportive administrative team and leadership  

- Opportunities for professional development and growth  

- Community and Lifestyle: Experience the charm of family-oriented communities with beautiful neighborhoods, top-notch public and private schools, and a strong sense of community spirit.   

Just minutes away from the bustling Charlotte metro area, you’ll have access to fabulous shopping, gourmet dining, exciting professional sporting events, concerts, and cultural experiences. Plus, with Charlotte Douglas International Airport nearby, traveling is a breeze!

Enjoy a four-season climate with opportunities for outdoor recreation. Lakes, mountains, and beaches are all just a short drive away, perfect for those who love to explore nature and seek adventure.
