        • Nurse Practitioner, Electrophysiology- Atlantic Health Morristown Medical Center
          • Locations
            Morristown, NJ, United States
          • Posting Date
            07/16/2026
          Be the First to Apply

          Atlantic Health, one of New Jersey’s largest nonprofit healthcare organizations, is seeking an Electrophysiology Advanced Practice Provider (NP or PA) to join the Cardiac Services team at Morristown Medical Center. This is an exciting opportunity to work alongside a highly regarded team of Electrophysiologists and Cardiologists in a collaborative, patient-centered environment. The APP will play a key role in the management of patients with cardiac arrhythmias and electrophysiology conditions across the continuum of care. Responsibilities include conducting pre- and post-procedure evaluations, managing patients with cardiac implantable electronic devices, providing care for patients undergoing catheter ablations and left atrial appendage occlusion (LAAO) procedures, and facilitating admissions and discharges. The ideal candidate will have prior cardiology or electrophysiology experience and be comfortable delivering comprehensive patient care in both procedural and clinical.
