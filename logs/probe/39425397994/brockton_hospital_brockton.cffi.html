Welcome to Signature Healthcare

For over 125 years, Signature Healthcare has proudly served as the leading healthcare provider in the communities of Southeastern Massachusetts. Our not-for-profit, integrated healthcare system brings together the award-winning Signature Healthcare Brockton Hospital and Signature Medical Group (SMG) to create a seamless patient experience with more than 150 physicians across 15 locations. This collaborative approach ensures the residents of Bridgewater, Brockton, East Bridgewater, North Easton, Raynham, Stoughton, and surrounding communities receive comprehensive, coordinated care close to home.

What sets Signature Healthcare apart is our team approach to medicine. Our highly skilled physicians, nurses, and healthcare professionals work together across multiple specialties and locations, communicating and collaborating to deliver exceptional care through a wide range of medical services to patients of all ages. This distinctive approach leverages advanced technologies, partnerships, and resources to make a meaningful difference in the health and lives of our patients. Our commitment to excellence has earned us national recognition for quality and safety, a testament to the exceptional care patients receive from our dedicated medical staff.

Brockton Hospital School of Nursing is also a vital part of Signature Healthcare. Established in 1897, the co-educational institution offers a prestigious nursing education and is the only hospital-based nursing school in Massachusetts.
