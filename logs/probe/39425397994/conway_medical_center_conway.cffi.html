Careers

Your pathway to the career & life you desire

As the first hospital in one of the quickest growing areas in the U.S., Conway Medical Center continues to expand and add to our health system to improve access and the quality of healthcare available to our community members.  Located in the center of Horry County in the historic, charming town of Conway, CMC is minutes from the shores of the Grand Strand.  This is the place to live, work and raise a family.

Join the Region’s Top Healthcare Employer

Discover why Conway Medical Center has been recognized by Forbes as America’s Best-in-State Employer for three consecutive years and stands as the highest-ranked healthcare employer in the Grand Strand region. With over 2,500 team members strong, CMC offers exceptional career opportunities across diverse paths – from clinical roles in nursing, surgery, emergency medicine, and specialty care to non-clinical positions in administration, IT, finance, human resources, facilities, and more.

Whether you’re a healthcare professional looking to advance your clinical expertise or seeking to make an impact in healthcare through support services, CMC provides the resources, professional development opportunities, and workplace culture where you can build a meaningful career while making a difference in your community’s health.

Ready to join an award-winning team that values your growth and contributions? Explore our current opportunities and discover your next career move at Conway Medical Center.
