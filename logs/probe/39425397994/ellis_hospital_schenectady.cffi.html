Career Opportunities at Ellis Medicine

 

At Ellis Medicine, you’ll find more than just a job. You’ll discover a place where your skills are valued, your growth is supported, and your impact is felt — every single day.

We are a not-for-profit, 438-bed community and teaching healthcare system proudly serving New York’s Capital Region. With four main campuses and a dedicated team of over 3,000 colleagues, Ellis delivers exceptional, compassionate care across every stage of life.

Why Join Ellis Medicine?

  • A dynamic, high-performance team committed to clinical excellence and collaboration
  • Top-tier compensation and a comprehensive benefits package
  • A culture that champions professional development, personal well-being, and work-life balance
  • Opportunities to grow in an environment where nurses and all colleagues thrive

Whether you're just starting your career in healthcare or looking to take your next step, Ellis Medicine is a place to grow, lead, and make a difference.
