Hiring Process: What to Expect

After you submit an application or talk to a recruiter, we'll contact you to schedule an interview if you seem like a good fit.

  • 01 Application
  • 02 Interview
  • 03 Offer
  • 04 Pre-Employment Appointment
  • Tips for a Successful Interview
    • Be confident.
    • Avoid rescheduling.
    • Arrive on time and in the proper location for your interview.
    • Know who you are interviewing with. 
    • Research the job you're interviewing for. 
    • Have questions prepared. 
    • Have a positive and enthusiastic attitude. 
    • Dress in business attire. 
    • Bring copies of your resume. 
    • Treat everyone you encounter with respect. 
    • Practice good manners and body language. 
    • Speak clearly. 
    • Be prepared with specific examples of your past experiences. 
    • Wait for the interviewer to discuss salary and benefits.
    • Respond truthfully. 
    • Keep responses concise and focused. 
    • Do not complain about previous employers. 
    • Be true to yourself and authentic. 
    • Thank the interviewer. 

    Next, expect to hear a decision from us as soon as possible.

  • Next Steps

    Once you accept an offer from AnMed, you must complete a pre-employment physical and background screening.

    Plan to schedule a pre-employment appointment. It usually takes 90 minutes to two hours. Before your appointment, you'll receive emails from Human Resources and the Employee Health Clinic. These emails include:

    • Documents and forms to complete and bring to your appointment
    • List of items to bring to your appointment

    Your appointment will include: 

    • Visit to Human Resources, where you'll submit and review your documentation, complete a direct deposit form and receive a parking decal 
    • Physical examination at the Employee Health Clinic, which includes a drug screen and potentially blood work depending on your immunization records

    Wear comfortable clothes and shoes. Your exam may include a job function test based on the requirements of your position. The test may include squats, pushing and pulling, lifting a weighted box, etc.

COVID-19 Vaccination

Notice:  At AnMed, all new hires must be fully vaccinated against COVID-19 prior to start date or have a medical or religious exemption request approved by AnMed.  Proof of vaccination or approved exemption must be provided during pre-hire health screening.  Exemption requests may delay your start date.

Questions?

During your job search or hiring process, email jobs@anmed.org if you:

  • Have a question about working at AnMed or a specific position
  • Need help using our website or jobs portal

Privacy Disclaimer

AnMed will never ask for any personal or financial information via email, direct messages, or any social media platform. All AnMed employee emails will end in @anmed.org or @anmedhealthcannon.org. In addition, AnMed will never offer a remote position and request any type of payment or send the candidate a check for equipment. If you have any questions or concerns, contact the Human Resources Office at 864-512-3886.
