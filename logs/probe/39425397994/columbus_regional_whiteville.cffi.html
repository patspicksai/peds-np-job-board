At Columbus Regional Healthcare System, our Mission is to provide compassionate care, advance healing, and inspire hope for our patients and their loved ones. Part of advancing healing is to protect the health and well-being of every patient we serve.

Who We Are:

Columbus Regional Healthcare System is a 154-bed, not-for-profit organization affiliated with Atrium Health (formerly Carolinas Healthcare System) and accredited by the DNV GL Healthcare Public Notice. CRHS boasts an impressive and highly trained medical staff and other healthcare professionals dedicated to the well-being of every patient in their care.

What We Offer:

  • Competitive pay rates
  • Matching retirement plan
  • Employee-oriented workplace
  • Tuition reimbursement
  • Comprehensive benefit package
  • Professional growth and advancement
  • Small hospital feel with large healthcare system resources
  • Sign-on bonus for select positions

Where We Are:

Located in Whiteville, NC just 45 miles west of Wilmington and 45 miles north of Myrtle Beach, the region around CRHS has something for everyone.

  • Small towns with Southern charm
  • Galleries and museums
  • Shopping and restaurants
  • World-class golf courses
  • Beautiful Atlantic beaches
  • Winding rivers and picturesque lakes
  • Expansive outdoor space for hiking and camping
