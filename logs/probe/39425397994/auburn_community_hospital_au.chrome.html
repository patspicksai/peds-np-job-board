Careers

Thank you for your interest in a career at Auburn Community Hospital.

Our employees are devoted professionals dedicated to providing quality, compassionate care for patients, family members, employees and the community at large. Healthcare and other professionals have the chance to build their careers as well as the healthcare system. Each member of our multidisciplinary team is an important contributor. We also offer a variety of non-clinical career paths, and a work environment where quality is valued and so are you.

Auburn Community Hospital is an equal-opportunity, affirmative-action employer. Auburn Community Hospital prohibits discrimination and harassment based on race, color, creed, religion, sex, gender, national origin, citizenship, ethnicity, marital status, age, disability, sexual orientation, gender identity and gender expression, veteran status, or any other status protected by applicable law to the extent prohibited by law.

If you are interested in applying your skills in a setting that values every employee, we welcome you to learn more about us and how you can become a part of our staff in providing quality, compassionate care.
