About Iredell Health System

What began in 1954 as a small community hospital has grown into a thriving nonprofit health system that is locally owned and managed. Today, Iredell Health System includes Iredell Memorial Hospital, Iredell Davis Behavioral Health Hospital, Iredell Mooresville, Iredell Home Health, Iredell Wound Care & Hyperbaric Center, Community and Corporate Wellness, Occupational Medicine, the Iredell Physician Network and more. The mission of Iredell Health System is to Inspire Wellbeing – Together.

Our history

Mooresville Campus

Urgent Care, Occupational Medicine, Imaging, Rehab, and Surgery services are included in the 68,000 square foot building that makes up Phase One of our 17.20 acre campus at 653 Bluefield Road. Dr. Judith Prairie and Christopher Upchurch, FNP-C see patients of all ages at Family Care Center of Mooresville. Cardiologists also see patients at that practice. Also at Iredell Mooresville are Stout Internal Medicine & Wellness, the practice of Dr. Sam Stout and Andrea Colvin, FNP-C, and Iredell Pain Management with Dr. John Edmiston and Dr. Rob Theiss.
