  • GET THE RIGHT CARE... IN THE RIGHT PLACE

    The Emergency Department is not always the ideal site for every health care need. We will always be there for you, but please, reserve the ED for the most critical injuries and illnesses. For routine care, avoid the wait and try one of our walk-in sites. Know your options.  [more]

  • Ellis Performs Capital Region Cardiac First

  • Breast Cancer Awareness Month at Ellis Medicine

    When it comes to breast cancer, early detection can make all the difference. That's why during the month of October, Ellis Medicine's annual Mammothon campaign continues to encourage women across the Capital Region to prioritize their breast health and schedule potentially lifesaving screenings.  [more]

    Events & Calendar

    Mammothon

    October

    Early detection is the best way to treat breast cancer. We’re dedicated to helping all women improve their chances of survival. To schedule an appointment at Bellevue Woman's (Breast Health) Center, call the Scheduling Department for Breast Care Services at 518-246-1000 or use the Request an Appointment button.  [more]

    High School Community Day

    Monday, Nov. 2 | 4:30 - 6:30 PM

    Community Day at Ellis Medicine is designed for high school students in grades 9-12 who want to learn more about careers in healthcare. Explore different career paths, connect with professionals, participate in hands-on activities, and discover possibilities for your future. Click to sign up!  [more]

    Fall Family Boo Bash

    Saturday, Oct 31 | 11 AM - 2 PM

    Ellis Medicine and Hometown Health Centers are teaming up to host the Fall Family Boo Bash! Bring the family for trick or treating, games, face painting, and other activities - and a chance to meet the teams behind the care we provide in our community.  [more]
