Our medical teams have been a part of the HCA Healthcare family for years. Uniting under one name to expand our network and resources, we are now HCA Healthcare Colleton Hospital. We will continue to meet the diverse needs of our community through specialized services like physical rehabilitation, labor and delivery support and minimally invasive surgery.

Featured services

We are proud to offer comprehensive healthcare services and programs to you and your family. Some of our specialties include:

Emergency care

When the unexpected happens, our emergency department offers 24/7 treatment. Our board-certified physicians are equipped to treat injuries, infections and complex conditions when you need it most.

Our emergency services

Labor and delivery

Our birthing unit provides you and your family with comfortable delivery suites and a dedicated postpartum unit. Our compassionate team of obstetrician-gynecologists (OB/GYNs) and nurses prioritize taking care of you and your little one.

Labor and delivery services

Surgical care

Our surgeons perform general, joint replacement, gynecological and urological surgeries. We use minimally invasive techniques, such as robot-assisted surgery, whenever possible. This technique offers many potential benefits, such as a quicker recovery, reduced scarring and shorter hospital stays.

Surgical services
