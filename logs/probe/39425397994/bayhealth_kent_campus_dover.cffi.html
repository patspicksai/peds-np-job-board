Medicaid Changes are Coming!

Do these things now to get ready for new federal Medicaid changes:

  • Go to the Delaware Medicaid site, Medicaid ENGAGE – DHSS , and create a My ASSIST account.
  • Download the FREE MyDEMedicalAssistance mobile app – find it on the Apple App or Google Play store.
  • Make sure DE Medicaid has your current address and phone number.
  • Read all mail from the Department of Health and Social Services (DHSS).
  • Sign up for emails and text messages.

Recruitment Scam Alert

Please be cautious of recruitment scams. Bayhealth will never offer employment without a formal application and interview process.

To verify legitimate job opportunities, please visit Bayhealth.org/Careers.
