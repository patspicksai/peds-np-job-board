    About Us

    Established in 1946, CaroMont Regional Medical Center is a 476-bed hospital in Gastonia, NC. Nationally recognized for quality care and patient safety, the hospital offers a Level III Trauma Center, a Pediatric Care Center, a nationally accredited Cancer Center, The Birthplace and a Level III neonatal intensive care unit (NICU).

    The hospital also serves as the anchor of CaroMont Health's network of emergency departments and hospitals across Gaston County and the CaroMont Health service area.
