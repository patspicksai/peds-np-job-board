Comprehensive mental health services since 1834

At the Brattleboro Retreat, we are committed to excellence in compassionate mental health care — grounded in scientific evidence and centered on you. Here on our safe, restorative Vermont campus, your well-being motivates us while your courage inspires us.

Brattleboro Retreat: Excellence in Care, Innovation in Healing
