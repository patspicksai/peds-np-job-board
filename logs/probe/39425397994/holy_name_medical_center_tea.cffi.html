100 years of breakthroughs,
milestones and healing.

This is Holy Name's centennial year. The year in which we celebrate a lifetime of life-saving medicine and life-changing care. From the beginning, Holy Name has been equal parts innovation and compassion. Science and humanity. The source of great medicine and soul purpose. As we enter our next hundred years, we remain devoted to providing a healthier future for all we serve.

10 firsts (and so many more).

1

First U.S. hospital to offer medication to prevent RH disease, which causes anemia in newborns

2

First in the world to use Regeneron’s antibody cocktail for COVID-19

3

One of the first hospitals in the world to offer nonsurgical treatment of lung cancer

4

First Multiple Sclerosis Center in the region, now a national leader

5

One of the first to use AI technology for use in imaging and diagnostics

6

First in NJ to implant a dissolvable stent for peripheral artery disease

7

Among first in NJ to perform robotic spinal fusion surgery with imaging

8

Only hospital in NJ to offer clinical trial for a groundbreaking drug to reduce MS progression

9

First in U.S. to offer new immunotherapy treatment for endometrial cancer

10

First in U.S. hospital to offer a targeted therapy for platinum-resistant ovarian cancer
