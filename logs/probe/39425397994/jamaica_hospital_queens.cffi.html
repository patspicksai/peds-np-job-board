Careers

Explore exciting job opportunities at JHMC, a leading hospital in Queens, New York.

We are seeking highly skilled and dedicated candidates who are interested in the opportunity to make a significant contribution to our patients and community.

 

 

Jamaica Hospital Medical Center is part of the MediSys Health Network, which includes facilities in both Brooklyn and Queens. If you are looking for a position that aligns with your experience and background at Jamaica Hospital, and do not find one currently available, you can also search for opportunities at our other locations.

 

 

Apply for Employment Opportunities at Medisys Careers for Jamaica Hospital Medical Center and Flushing Hospital Medical Center

 

It is the policy and practice of this institution to ensure equal employment opportunities to employees and applicants without discrimination or harassment on the basis of race, color, national origin, religion, sex, age, disability, alienage or citizenship status, martial status, creed, genetic predisposition or carrier status, sexual orientation, gender identity, or any other characteristic protected by law. The institution is in compliance with the Americans with Disabilities Act (ADA) of 1992.
