Penn State Health Careers

Changing Lives through Hearts and Minds – Together.

At Penn State Health, our approach to health care is bigger than all of us. It calls us to learn, achieve, share and lift each other up. Because the hearts and minds of many, together, can make the most impact.

Job Search

Health Care That’s Personal –
The Human Connection

Ours is a commitment to health care that is personal. Because we know when we change the lives of patients, we are also changed. It means connecting with patients—not only with their medical needs and concerns, but also with their hopes and dreams.

Whether we provide care directly or support those who do, together, we pursue a singular goal: To give every patient the health they need to live the life they want.
