Our Mission

Dartmouth Health provides world-class care woven into the fabric of our communities. We advance health through research, education, clinical practice, and partnerships—providing each person the best care, in the right place, at the right time, every time.

Our Vision

We strive to ensure the most advanced medical care is accessible to our patients and families—wherever they are—while setting the highest standards in healthcare delivery.

The Dartmouth Health Family
