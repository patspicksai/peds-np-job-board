Excellence in Action Jade Kelley-Pate, RN, was caring for patients in the Emergency Department when a teenage girl arrived in severe abdominal pain. From the moment she entered the room, Jade brought calm and clarity. She spent 10 hours with the family, taking time to ask the patient about prom, summer plans, and college dreams. 

That connection left a lasting impression. The patient’s mother later wrote: “My daughter said, ‘That is the type of nurse I want to be. Someone who makes people feel comfortable and safe.’”
Our Family-Centered Approach When Avery, a young patient diagnosed with leukemia, was admitted to CHOP, her family hoped she'd be discharged in time for her uncle’s wedding. She was looking forward to being the flower girl. 

A team of compassionate staff sprang into action. The unforgettable day began with a surprise visit from Avery’s uncle, dressed in his tux for photos. Later, Avery joined the wedding via FaceTime, tossing petals down an empty hallway lined with battery-operated candles. She even made invitations for staff to join a reception—complete with music and snacks—in her room. 
Our Culture of Kindness
When Security Officer Joseph Tyler found an unhoused man barefoot and struggling in the freezing weather, he retrieved an extra pair of sneakers he had packed for the gym, helped the man put on socks and shoes, tied his laces, and helped him back to his feet.

The moment was witnessed by another member of the CHOP community, Sarah Wolf, who was moved by Joseph's compassion. While he insisted that anyone on his team would have done the same, his actions serve as a powerful reminder that our values are often reflected in simple acts of humanity that make a lasting impact.
