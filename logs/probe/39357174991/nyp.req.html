Celebrating Nursing Excellence
Across All of Our Hospitals

At NewYork-Presbyterian, we don’t just aim for excellence—we achieve it. All of our hospitals have earned Magnet® recognition, the nation’s highest honor for nursing excellence. We’re proud to be the only healthcare system in New York State with this designation across every hospital. With fewer than 10% of U.S. hospitals holding Magnet status, this achievement reflects our nurses’ professionalism, teamwork, and commitment to superior patient care.
