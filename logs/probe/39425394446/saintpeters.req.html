At Saint Peter's Healthcare System, we are deeply committed to providing humble service, compassionate care and medical excellence to our communities every day. The dedication of our talented team and the warm, welcoming culture we offer will inspire you to share your greatest talents and flourish. Through the mission you help us deliver and the meaningful connections you'll make, Saint Peter's will become the home you choose for your career.

Saint Peter's Healthcare System has been serving the community for 117 years.

Our dedicated team consists of more than 1,000 physicians and 3,600 employees.

We are the fifth hospital in the world to earn a 7th consecutive Magnet® designation and proud to receive our first Magnet with Distinction® designation

We’re recognized as a Best Hospital by Women’s Choice Award

Our Pediatric Emergency department was the first in NJ to receive the Lantern Award, and has once again earned this prestigious recognition.

Our CARES Surgicenter helps over 10,000 children and adults requiring same-day surgery each year.
