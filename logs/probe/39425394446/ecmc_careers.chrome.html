Careers at ECMC

ECMC has over 4,000 caring and dedicated employees who make a difference every day in the lives of our patients, their families, and our community.

What makes us different?

With a wide variety of services, including our Level 1 Adult Trauma Center, Comprehensive Psychiatric Emergency Program, Transplant Surgery, Long-Term Care, and more, we are able to provide a range of opportunities for new and existing employees. Whether you’re actively looking for the next step in your healthcare career or exploring your options, our opportunities and benefits make ECMC a great place to work.

Apply Today
  • Competitive wages and shift differentials ($6/hr for evening and night shift RNs)
  • Paid lunches and breaks
  • Free parking
  • Free Fitness Center and Wellness Program
  • Paid time off
  • Premium pay for holidays
  • Medical and Rx Insurance – PPO plan with low co-pays and no deductible
  • Dental Insurance
  • Vision Coverage
  • Flexible Spending Accounts including a Healthcare Reimbursement Account, Dependent Care Account, and Premium Reimbursement Account
  • Pension through the New York State Retirement System with life insurance benefit
  • Retirement savings: 457b Deferred Compensation
  • Employee Assistance Program
  • Comprehensive clinical orientation
  • Development and career advancement opportunities
  • Work culture focused on fostering a positive, inclusive and diverse environment

Physician Career Opportunities

Erie County Medical Center, located in Buffalo, NY, offers physician career opportunities across 25+ specialties, and primary care. ECMCC is a verified Level 1 Trauma Center and the major teaching hospital for the University of Buffalo Medical School. Because of our proximity to the Canadian border, ECMCC frequently sponsors J1/H1(b) immigration waiver candidates.

Learn More

Residency Opportunities

As a major teaching facility for the State University of New York at Buffalo, ECMC offers an atmosphere where new doctors work alongside today’s experts to gain critical experience and take patient care to new heights. Through our programs, residents develop the skills to help our patients and community thrive.
