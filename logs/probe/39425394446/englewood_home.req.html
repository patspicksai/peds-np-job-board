Are you ready to make a positive impact for your community?

Whether you are interested in patient care, technical roles, administrative support, management, or another career area, your journey starts now.

When you join our team at Englewood Health, you become part of a health system dedicated to transforming lives—including your own.

With more than 100 locations throughout northern New Jersey, Englewood Health offers opportunities to change the lives of countless people in our communities. And our work environment is one that promotes growth and learning, embraces diversity, and rewards excellence.

Why choose Englewood Health for your career?

A growing health system

Englewood Health is a dynamic health system with a 130-year legacy of serving our community. Over the past decade, we have grown and expanded into more communities.

Locations across the region

With 100+ outpatient locations and physician practices in five counties—Bergen, Hudson, Passaic, Essex, and Morris—and our flagship inpatient hospital in Englewood, you can bring your passion to a location that serves you best.

A culture of excellence

We provide award-winning patient care—as well as an award-winning workplace environment. In 2023, Englewood Health was named to Becker’s Hospital Review 150 Best Places to Work in Healthcare (ranked first in New Jersey) and Forbes America’s Best-in-State Employers.

Full range of benefits

Employees can take advantage of comprehensive benefits—including health insurance, life insurance, retirement savings plan, tuition assistance, paid time off, wellness services, and much more.

Commitment to diversity

Our team includes individuals from all walks of life and backgrounds. We stand strongly committed to diversity, equity, and inclusion among our employees as well as patients and community.

Professional growth

Through tuition assistance, professional development courses, mentorship programs, and opportunities for promotion, Englewood Health offers an environment of continuous growth, learning, and advancement.
