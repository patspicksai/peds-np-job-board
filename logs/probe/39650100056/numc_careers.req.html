Careers at NuHealth

 

We’re committed to quality, diversity, education and community.
If that sounds like your kind of workplace, we have a variety of positions for direct patient care professionals and others in our three Centers of Care:

  • Nassau University Medical Center
  • A. Holly Patterson Extended Care Facility
  • NuHealth Family Health Centers

For other general employment information, e-mail Human Resources at careers@numc.edu or fax us at (516) 572-0089.

EMPLOYMENT SCAM WARNING:

It has come to our attention that people pretending to be NuHealth recruiters have been offering fake work-from-home employment opportunities with NuHealth. NuHealth does not recruit via text/e-mail messaging and candidates for employment are always interviewed in-person at our offices in Nassau County, NY.

 

If you receive a suspicious text/e-mail solicitation in connection with potential employment with us, do not disclose any personal information to the sender and do not send or accept any money.  Instead, you are encouraged to contact your local law enforcement agency and forward the suspicious email to the Office of Legal Affairs, numc_legal@numc.edu.
