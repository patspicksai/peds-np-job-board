    Advance Your Career at Rutland Regional Medical Center

    Are you looking for a rewarding healthcare career? Look no further than Rutland Regional Medical Center located in the beautiful Green Mountains of Vermont.

    Check out our job opportunities and learn more about what makes Rutland Regional such a unique and rewarding place to pursue a hospital career. From nursing to environmental services, food and nutrition to administrative opportunities, Rutland Regional boasts a variety of clinical and non-clinical jobs where you can pursue a rewarding career in healthcare.

    Why Work at Rutland Regional?

    We’ve built our reputation for excellence by hiring, training and retaining the most caring healthcare professionals in the industry and we’re dedicated to the success of our team. Our commitment to the communities we serve and to the development of our employees’ careers embody what it means to be the best.

    Rutland Regional has received many awards for quality and employee engagement over the years, including:

    • Advisory Board 2018 Workplace of the Year Award
    • High-performing hospital by U.S. News & World Report
    • Healthgrades named us one of America’s 100 Best Hospitals for Joint Replacement
    • ANCC Magnet® recognized hospital continuously since 2010

    Equal Opportunity Employer

    Rutland Regional Medical Center is an Equal Opportunity Employer. It is our policy to provide employment, training, compensation, promotion and other conditions of employment without regard to race, color, national origin, place of birth, gender, sexual orientation, religion, marital status, age, disability, protected veteran status or obligation for service in the armed forces.

    At Rutland Regional, we understand that our strength and success comes from our award-winning team, a team dedicated to taking care of others. Which is why it's important to us to take care of you.
