Why join our community?

Hospital for Special Care (HFSC) is recognized for advanced care and rehabilitation in pulmonary care, acquired brain injury, medically-complex pediatrics, neuromuscular disorders, spinal cord injury, comprehensive heart failure and comprehensive inpatient and outpatient treatment for children and adolescents with autism spectrum disorder.

Top Workplace

Our employees are passionate and think highly about their work here. For the thirteenth year in a row, we were named as one of the large business division “Hartford Courant CT Top Workplaces”.

Top Workplace USA

We have earned the Top Workplaces USA award for the fourth year in a row, for building a great working culture.

Women-Led Top Workplace

We are honored as a Woman-Led Top Workplace for the second consecutive year. This award is given to organizations who's senior most woman leader fosters a caring, innovative and rewarding workplace culture.

Healthcare Industry Top Workplace

The Top Workplaces Healthcare Industry Award recognizes exceptional workplace culture, highlighting the hospital's commitment to employee satisfaction and patient care excellence.
