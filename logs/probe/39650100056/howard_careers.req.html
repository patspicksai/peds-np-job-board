Welcome Home Career Fair

Howard University Hospital (HUH) invites nursing and allied health professionals to join us for the Welcome Home Career Fair on Tuesday, October 13, 2026, from 10:00 a.m. to 3:00 p.m. in the Towers Building Auditorium.

​

As Howard University Hospital continues to grow and prepare for an exciting future, we are seeking talented, compassionate healthcare professionals who share our commitment to clinical excellence, innovation and community-centered care.

​

This recruitment event offers attendees an opportunity to meet directly with hiring managers, discover available positions, connect with our recruitment team and learn about career advancement opportunities at HUH.

​

Whether you're an experienced healthcare professional or seeking the next step in your career, discover how your skills and passion can make a meaningful difference in the lives of our patients and the communities we serve.​

About HUH Careers

For more than 160 years, Howard University Hospital has been advancing healthcare, educating future medical leaders, and serving our community with excellence and compassion.

 

As the nation's only teaching hospital located on the campus of a historically Black university, HUH offers extraordinary opportunities to make an impact while building a meaningful career in one of Washington, D.C.'s leading academic medical centers.

HUH Employee Spotlight

HUH Careers Web Portal

At Howard University Hospital, our people are at the heart of everything we do. Why I Chose Howard shares the voices and experiences of employees who have chosen to build their careers here. Through personal testimonials, team members highlight what brought them to Howard, what inspires them to stay, and how their work contributes to our legacy of excellence, service, innovation, and compassionate care. Discover what it means to be part of a community where your career can make a difference—in the lives of our patients, our community, and beyond.

Why I Chose Howard

Back to the Top

A Legacy of Service

“I saw a growth in coming here and learning a lot of basically different areas from the hospital to work in. I started as a call center supervisor, then worked my way in to supervise other area clinics. I just like the staff here throughout the years, meeting different people. Even working with residents, seeing them come and graduate. So that kept me here.”
Doris Nava, Patient Access Supervisor (16 years)

Growing My Career

“What inspired me to start at Howard was the opportunity to provide higher education for my kids to attend college. This has inspired me to stay here and take advantage of the opportunities. I started as a boy and have grown into a man over the years with responsibility. I have come this far and I am not going anywhere. I just want to thank God for allowing me to be here for 26 years. I appreciate it.” 
Julius Moore, Patient Transportation (26 years).

Making A Difference

“I received my administrative experience starting at Medgar Evers College in Brooklyn, New York, and I wanted to continue my career at another hospital, which happened to be HUH. I am passionate about helping my fellow employees. Everybody is so kind, everybody is dedicated. Dedicated to their careers. Dedicated to this institution. I think I was born to be an administrator. This is what I do. And being in the medical field, it helps me with my everyday life. With employees, my family at home. I just came natural to me.”

Ingrid Figaro, Administrative Assistant II (14 years).
