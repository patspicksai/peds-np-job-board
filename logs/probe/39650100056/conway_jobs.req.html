Join our CMC Team

Considering a job on our team?

Your commitment:

I will exemplify the CMC Core Values of Excellence, Teamwork, Compassion, Healing, Integrity, Innovation and Stewardship every day.

I will promote and foster a welcoming culture that is representative of CMC’s commitment to diversity and inclusion.

I will focus on quality and the CMC mission of improving the health of our communities no matter the position I hold at CMC.

I am willing to receive the required annual influenza (flu) vaccine and the COVID-19 vaccine.

I accept and value that CMC is a Drug Free Workplace that completes new team member testing and current team member ongoing monthly random testing.

I understand that I may need to demonstrate that I am physically capable of performing the essential physical functions of the position I am applying for through a Physical Competency Assessment.

I understand that CMC will confirm my background, education, and qualifications through a thorough background review

Sign me up!
