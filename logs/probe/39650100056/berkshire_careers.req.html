What’s great about living in the Berkshires?

Berkshire Health Systems invites you to enjoy the benefits of a close-knit, supportive community that fosters professional growth and personal well-being. The Berkshires offer top-tier schools, year-round outdoor recreation, and a rich array of cultural experiences, ensuring a high quality of life. Don’t take our word for it! Here’s what our providers and advanced practice providers say they love most about the Berkshires.

Make the Right Call
The Berkshire Health System Nurse Line is a free community service that can help connect you to the care you need. Our nurses will answer questions and guide you to the right services at the right time – in the office, at Urgent Care, or in the Emergency Room. 

Call 855-855-2000 today.

Berkshire Health Systems
Please contact the health system’s switchboard for general information, to be connected to an inpatient room, or to be connected to a program or service. For specific appointment questions, call your provider’s office directly.
