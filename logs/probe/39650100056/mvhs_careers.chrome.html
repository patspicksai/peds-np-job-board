Notice of Job Offer

Neuro Critical Care Physician (MVHS, Inc, Utica, NY): Diagnose and treat life-threatening diseases of the nervous system, including brain and spinal cord trauma, cerebral edema, ruptured aneurysms, severe strokes, encephalitis and meningitis, refractory seizures, and complicated brain tumors. Manage medical complications that may occur in patients, and deal with ethical and social issues including estimating prognosis, planning advance directives, and counseling patients and their families. Requires: M.D. (First Professional Medical degree), Neurology residency, Neuro Critical Care fellowship, and a New York State medical license or eligibility. Salary: $373,000.00 per year. Apply: Submit CV and credentials to Joelle Mead, Director of Physician Recruitment, 111 Hospital Drive, Utica, NY 13502.

Notice of Job Offer

EMPLOYER: MVHS, Inc.

JOB TITLE: Lead Medical Technologist

JOB DESCRIPTION: Perform clinical testing in at least two of the following areas: blood bank, microbiology, serology, chemistry, hematology, or urinalysis. Obtain and receive specimens. Prepare samples for analysis. Evaluate suitability of specimen for analysis according to laboratory
policy. Evaluate and report laboratory results. Maintain clinical laboratory instrumentation/equipment. Conduct quality control procedures. Maintain stock levels, conduct laboratory instrumentation performance checks and calibrations, train other staff and perform competency assessments. Multiple positions available.

MINIMUM REQUIREMENTS: Bachelor’s degree in Medical Technology or related science, and New York State Clinical Laboratory Technologist License or eligibility.

RATE OF PAY: $42.17/hour - $49.06/hour

JOB LOCATIONS: Wynn Hospital, 111 Hospital Drive, Utica, NY 13502

Apply: Submit CV to:

Rylee Maxam
International Program Specialist
201 Lafayette St
Utica, NY 13502
