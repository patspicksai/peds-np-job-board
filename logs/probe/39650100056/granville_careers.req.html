Working in Granville County

Located just north of the Triangle area, Granville County is made up of communities like Oxford, Butner, and Creedmoor. While residents of Granville County enjoy a relaxing rural lifestyle, the close proximity of major cities like Raleigh and Durham allows easy access to the benefits of city life, as well.
