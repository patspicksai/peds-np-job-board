As a leading regional employer, Mount Nittany Health employs over 2,400 team members. Along with competitive salaries and excellent benefits, our employees enjoy being part of an award-winning team. From physicians at Mount Nittany Medical Center to support staff in our many outpatient locations, we value our employees and the experience they bring.

Mount Nittany Health is dedicated to our mission of “Healthier people, stronger community.”

We invite you to explore the possibilities and join our team today!

Kathleen Rhine, President and CEO, Mount Nittany Health

Welcome

Since our beginning in 1902, the system has grown to include our parent organization, Mount Nittany Health, along with Mount Nittany Medical Center, a 260-bed acute care facility, and Mount Nittany Physician Group, a practice with more than 220 healthcare providers, across 30 specialties, located in six counties throughout the region as well as its fundraising and development entity, Mount Nittany Health Foundation.

We strive to give our patients, our providers, and our staff a positive “Mount Nittany Health Experience,” embracing top-quality care, operational excellence, growth and innovation in the field of healthcare, as we continue to advance our mission healthier people, stronger community.

Once again, thank you for considering Mount Nittany Health as a place to begin or further your career.

Who we are:

Mount Nittany Health is the parent organization for:

  • Mount Nittany Medical Center
    A 260-bed acute care facility offering medical, surgical, diagnostic and community services
  • Mount Nittany Health Physician Group
    A group of over 220 healthcare providers, across more than 30 medical specialties
  • Mount Nittany Health Foundation
    The fundraising entity for Mount Nittany Health developments
  • Mount Nittany Health Children’s Advocacy Center
    A community-based, child-focused center that is one of over 40 centers nationally. It facilitates a compassionate, multidisciplinary approach to the prevention, identification, intervention, and treatment of child abuse.

The collaboration among these entities allows us to provide excellent patient care, with the latest technology and expert medical professionals. We are committed to improving the quality and availability of healthcare in our region. We do this by expanding facilities, recruiting the best providers, and adding services our patients need.
