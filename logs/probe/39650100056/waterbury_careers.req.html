UConn Health Community Network Careers

At UConn Health Community Network, we provide a diverse range of career options. From administrative, financial, service and maintenance, nursing and clinical careers, we offer varied opportunities for professional growth. At UConn Health Community Network, we prioritize fostering advancement, continuous learning, and maintaining a harmonious work-life balance for our team members.

  • UConn Health Community Network is a thriving community-based system of care serving a regional population of 280,000 patients. Our network employs more than 2,000 and supports a growing medical staff, currently close to 700 strong. Comprised of Waterbury Hospital a 347-bed community teaching facility affiliated with University of Connecticut School of Medicine and Connecticut Children's Medical Center, as well as UConn Health Community Network Medical Group, Access Rehab Centers and a leading partner in the Harold-Leever Cancer Center we have been at the cutting edge of healthcare in our region for more than 130 years.

    Waterbury Region
    Waterbury is the fifth largest city in Connecticut and is the home of an estimated 110,360 citizens of diverse ethnic, cultural, and religious backgrounds. Located along the I-84 corridor, our surrounding communities such as Southbury, Woodbury, Middlebury, Watertown, Cheshire, Wolcott, Southington; Prospect, and Naugatuck to name a few, offer wonderful lifestyle options and amenities in thriving family-based communities that provide easy access to New York and Boston, Connecticut's Gold Coast, and the Berkshires of Western Massachusetts.
